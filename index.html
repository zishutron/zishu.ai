About Zishu

Zishu is an AI voice assistant created to make interacting with technology feel more natural, simple and personal.

The Story Behind Zishu

Zishu was created from the idea of building a voice assistant that feels simple and natural to use. Instead of creating an experience that feels complicated or overwhelming, the goal was to make an assistant that users can simply open, interact with and talk to.

The project has been developed progressively, with continuous improvements to: voice interaction, AI conversations, user experience, multilingual support, responsive design, assistant tools, accessibility, and overall reliability.

Technology should feel useful, simple and accessible. Designed for Every Screen

Zishu is designed to work across mobile, tablet, laptop, and desktop. The interface remains clean, responsive and easy to use across different screen sizes.

Zishu is being designed to support users across different languages. Multilingual accessibility is an important part of the project's direction. Currently, the interface supports English and Hindi.
